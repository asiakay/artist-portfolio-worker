About

I’m an audiovisual artist and storyteller using digital tools to uncover patterns—how technology, culture, and memory shape perception. My work moves between experimental film, sound, and digital media, merging motion, design, and research to translate complex systems into human stories.

I explore systems through rhythm and structure, culture through remix and narrative, and design through the language of editing and sound. Each piece aims to make the invisible visible—to reveal not just the emotional architecture, but also the objective scaffolding that holds our collective experience together.
